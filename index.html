What DSF does

Fragmented sources in. Certified supply out.

Feedstock starts in many small places: kitchens, food processors, oil refining by-products, tyre yards. On its own, none of it is supply a refinery can contract against.

We source it, qualify it and consolidate it into certified volume for global markets. The model is the same whichever stream we add next, which is why DSF is defined by the system, not by any single commodity.

The DSF system

Value is created at every step.

Five activities, one operating system. Each feedstock stream takes its own route through it. Choose a stream to see its route.

Used cooking oil from households, food service and industry, aggregated into export-grade volume.

  1. Collect
  2. Aggregate
  3. Trade
  • 1

    Collect

    We source from fragmented local points: households, food service, industry and independent collectors.

  • Recover

    We recover usable feedstock from by-products and end-of-life materials.

  • Connect

    We bring local partners, collectors and small businesses into one qualified network.

  • 2

    Aggregate

    We consolidate small, scattered volumes into reliable, specification-grade lots.

  • 3

    Trade

    We supply certified feedstock to refineries and buyers in global markets.

Underneath every route: traceability, quality control and data. This layer is what makes the supply certified, not merely aggregated.

Four pillars

Why the supply holds.

  • The network

    Supply chain

    The physical infrastructure that moves feedstock from local sources to global markets: collect, aggregate, store, move, recover, trade.

  • The data

    Technology

    Traceability, qualification and data that make the supply defensible and the business scalable.

  • The people

    Social

    Households, local partners, independent collectors and small businesses enter the value chain. Measured participation that builds livelihoods and sourcing depth.

  • The standard

    Environment

    Waste diverted, resources recovered, CO₂ avoided. Expressed as numbers and chain of custody.

The network moves feedstock. The data makes it certified. The people make it defensible. The standard sets its direction.
